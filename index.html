The story

I'm Yeswanth, and I'm in my first year of B.Tech Information Technology in Chennai. Before college I was the kid who took things apart to see how they worked, and mostly couldn't put them back together.

Code turned out to be the one hobby where taking things apart is the whole point, and where you can undo your mistakes. That's what got me hooked.

Right now I'm at the start of the film, still building the basics, still making a mess. I'd rather show you something half-finished and honest than something polished that I can't explain.

I'm looking for people to learn with, projects to join, and a first internship when I'm ready for it.
